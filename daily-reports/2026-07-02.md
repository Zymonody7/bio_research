## B. Clinical Agent + RAG/Knowledge Graph

### 1. 🔥 AD-CARE: Guideline-grounded LLM Agent for Alzheimer's Diagnosis
**arXiv:2603.25322** | [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25322)

> Alzheimer's disease (AD) is a growing global health challenge. Real-world AD assessment is hampered by incomplete, heterogeneous multimodal data and variability across sites and demographics. Although LLMs have shown promise in biomedicine, their use in AD has largely been confined to narrow, disease-specific questions. **AD-CARE** introduces a guideline-grounded, modality-agnostic LLM agent for real-world AD diagnosis with multi-cohort assessment, fairness analysis, and reader study.

**Why it matters**: 首个面向阿尔茨海默病的多队列、多模态 LLM Agent 评估，包含公平性分析和临床医生可读性研究。

## C. RLHF Medical Alignment

### 3. 🔥🔥 MedHarm: Benchmark for High-Risk Medical Safety Alignment ⭐ TOP PICK
**arXiv:2606.28332** | [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28332)

> LLMs are increasingly used for medical questions, yet their safety in high-risk scenarios remains poorly understood. We introduce **MedHarm**, a high-risk medical safety benchmark with **1,100 medically grounded queries across 10 safety-critical categories**, including toxicology, pharmacology, and emergency medicine. Evaluates whether safety alignment techniques generalize to high-risk medical domains.

**Why it matters**: 填补了医疗高风险场景安全评估的空白，10 个安全关键类别覆盖全面。

### 4. 🔥 Dialectics of Alignment: Harnessing Unsafe Knowledge for Dynamic Safety Routing
**arXiv:2606.00686** | [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00686)

> The prevailing alignment paradigm operates via erasure, filtering unsafe data or training models to refuse harmful prompts. This approach fundamentally constricts the model's epistemological scope. We propose a **dialectical approach** that harnesses unsafe knowledge for dynamic safety routing rather than discarding it.

**Why it matters**: 挑战了"删除不安全数据"的主流范式，提出利用不安全知识进行动态路由。

### 5. 🔥 Safety Training Persists Through Helpfulness Optimization in LLM Agents
**arXiv:2603.02229** | [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02229)

> We study an **agentic (multi-step, tool-use)** setting where safety refers to harmful actions directly taken by the LLM. Comparing DPO on safety or helpfulness alone vs both sequentially. Surprisingly, safety can persist through helpfulness optimization, but with important caveats.

**Why it matters**: 首次系统研究 Agent 场景下的安全-有用性权衡。

### 6. 📖 The Neutral Mask: How RLHF Provides Shallow Alignment
**arXiv:2606.09735** | [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09735)

> RLHF shapes behavior by aligning with human values, yet the process is opaque. Evidence suggests RLHF produces only **functional compliance rather than deep alignment**. This work provides a mechanistic case study showing RLHF creates shallow alignment that can be circumvented.

**Why it matters**: 对 RLHF 有效性的批判性分析，对医疗 AI 安全有重要启示。

## D. Protein Language Models

### 7. 🔥 Protein Language Models Diverge from Natural Language
**arXiv:2602.20449** | [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20449)

> Modern PLMs apply transformer architectures from NLP to biological sequences. However, protein language has key differences from natural language, such as a rich functional space despite a vocabulary of only **20 amino acids**. These differences motivate research into how architectures operate differently in the protein domain.

**Why it matters**: 深入分析 PLM 与 NLP LM 的本质差异，为改进 PLM 架构提供理论指导。

### 8. 📎 CoPeP: Benchmarking Continual Pretraining for Protein LMs
**arXiv:2603.00253** | [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00253)

> We introduce **CoPeP**, a benchmark for continual pretraining of PLMs, curating protein datasets from UniProt spanning a decade to evaluate continual learning approaches across **31 protein understanding tasks**. Incorporating temporal meta-information improves perplexity by up to 7%.

**Why it matters**: 首个 PLM 持续预训练基准，揭示时间信息对蛋白质建模的价值。

### 9. 🔥 Inference-Time Toxicity Mitigation in Protein Language Models
**arXiv:2603.04045** | [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04045)

> PLMs are becoming practical tools for de novo protein design, yet their **dual-use potential** raises safety concerns. Domain adaptation to specific taxonomic groups can elicit toxic protein generation. We adapt **Logit Diff Amplification (LDA)** as an inference-time control mechanism for PLMs.

**Why it matters**: 生物安全 — PLM 可能被用于生成有毒蛋白质，本文提出推理时控制方法。
