## A. mNGS + AI 病原检测

### 🔥🔥 #1 Clinical pathogen adjudication in metagenomic sequencing using a large language model

- **期刊/来源**: medRxiv 预印本 (2026-10-16)
- **作者**: Muyun Wei, Yiming Zhao, Chao Fang, Shengyang Zhang, Ke Xu
- **标识**: DOI: `10.64898/2026.10.16.26361411`

> **一句话**: 把"哪个检出菌是真正致病菌"这一 mNGS 最痛的临床裁决环节交给 LLM——回顾 4,855 例标本、133 万个检出微生物-致病菌判定标签微调 LLM，留出集召回 92.44%，盲法复审 215 例中与双专家一致的裁决匹配 90.5%。

**摘要要点**: Clinical metagenomic sequencing detects hundreds of organisms whose relevance to infection requires clinicians' manual interpretation. 4,855 specimens from 19 sampling sites, 1.33 million detected organisms linked to culprit-pathogen determinations, fine-tuned LLM recovered 92.44% of reference culprit calls in held-out test set and 84.49% in a later cohort. In blinded re-adjudication of 215 cases, the model matched 90.5% of culprit calls on which both expert reviewers agreed. Adjudication accuracy varied with organism identity and specimen types. mNGS-derived metrics such as read counts or genome coverage alone were insufficient to identify culprit pathogens, and organisms classified as common colonizers could still be judged causative.

**推荐理由**: 直击 mNGS 临床落地最大瓶颈——"检出≠致病"的报告解读裁决。数据规模(4,855例/133万标签)与盲法复审设计罕见；发现 read counts/覆盖度单独不足以定罪、"常见定植菌"也可致病，与日常解读直觉一致。方向 A 与 LLM 的最强交叉。

### 📖 #2 Artificial Intelligence in Virus Detection: From Metagenomic Discovery to Clinical Decision Support and Population Surveillance

- **期刊/来源**: BioTech (Basel) (2026)
- **作者**: Liakopoulou G, Koutsiaris AG
- **标识**: DOI: `10.3390/biotech15040090` | PMID: `42930067`

> **一句话**: 综述：AI 病毒检测的三层架构——宏基因组层(短 contig/弱同源瓶颈下的 ViraLM 基因组基础模型、LucaProt 序列+结构双模 RdRP 高度分歧 RNA 病毒发现)、临床层(mNGS 与靶向检测互补)、人群层(多源数据预警系统)。

**摘要要点**: Reviews how AI supports virus detection across three interconnected levels. At the metagenomic level, AI addresses key bottlenecks caused by fragmented assemblies and weak homology signals. Foundation-model approaches such as ViraLM leverage pretrained genome representations to improve viral contig discrimination under short-contig conditions, whereas LucaProt integrates sequence- and structure-informed learning to detect highly divergent RNA viruses via RdRP-focused discovery. At the population level, early warning systems combine heterogeneous data sources.

**推荐理由**: 为 A 方向提供了"检测→临床→公卫"的完整框架地图，点名 ViraLM/LucaProt 等具体模型，适合作为追踪 A 方向模型谱系的索引文献。

## B. 临床智能体 + RAG/知识图谱

### 🔥🔥 #3 Agentic Automation of BT-RADS Scoring Using an End-to-End System for Standardized Glioma Follow-up Assessment

- **期刊/来源**: Radiology: Artificial Intelligence (2026)
- **作者**: Jabal MS, Zhang J, LaBella D, Houk JL, Zhang D, Rudie JD, Mazurowski MA, Magudia K, Calabrese E
- **标识**: DOI: `10.1148/ryai.260234` | PMID: `42905016`

> **一句话**: 多智能体 LLM+CNN 端到端系统自动化胶质瘤随访 MRI 的 BT-RADS 结构化评分：提取智能体(gpt-oss-20B)从病历抓临床变量 + 打分智能体整合体积测量执行决策逻辑，492 例可评估检查上系统准确率 76.0% vs 初始评估 57.5%(+18.5pp, P<0.001)，κ=0.71，BT-4 阳性预测值 92.9%。

**摘要要点**: An end-to-end multiagent LLM and CNN system for automated BT-RADS classification. 509 consecutive posttreatment glioma MRI examinations retrospectively evaluated. An extractor agent identified clinical variables from notes using gpt-oss-20B; a scorer agent applied BT-RADS decision logic integrating these with quantitative volumetrics. System accuracy 76.0% vs 57.5% for initial assessments (+18.5 pp, P<.001), Cohen kappa 0.71. High sensitivity in context-dependent categories (BT-1b 100%; BT-1a 92.7%), BT-4 PPV 92.9%.

**推荐理由**: 本期 B 方向最佳实战样本：不是"聊天机器人"而是把智能体嵌入真实影像随访流程，LLM(文本抽取)+CNN(体积测量)+规则引擎(评分逻辑)分工明确，并与放射科医师初始评估对照证明超越基线。Radiology 系列期刊背书。

### 🔥 #4 Graph-Enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Postoperative Patient Question Answering: A Comparative Evaluation of Knowledge Graph, RAG, and Hybrid GraphRAG Approaches

- **期刊/来源**: Bioengineering (Basel) (2026)
- **作者**: Prabha S, Collaco BG, Rosa Malena C, Haider SA, Wood N, Forte AJ
- **标识**: DOI: `10.3390/bioengineering13101097` | PMID: `42938952`

> **一句话**: 受控对比 KG-only vs RAG-only vs 混合 GraphRAG 在术后患者问答上的表现：250 道专家命题(含20道急诊意图、30道超范围)、3 名盲法临床医师评审，GraphRAG 96.8% > RAG-only 94.8% >> KG-only 56.0%——纯知识图谱路线显著落后。

**摘要要点**: Benchmark of 250 expert-authored questions evaluated on an identical knowledge corpus using Gemini 2.5 Flash for generation. Three blinded clinicians rated all 750 outputs. Overall accuracy 96.8% (GraphRAG), 94.8% (RAG-only), 56.0% (KG-only). GraphRAG pipeline includes query expansion, second retrieval stage, evidence fusion, and monotonic RAG fallback. Both retrieval-augmented systems outperformed KG-only.

**推荐理由**: 为"建知识图谱还是做检索增强"这一架构决策提供了少见的头对头证据——纯 KG 路线(56%)远逊于检索增强(≥94.8%)，但混合式仍小幅领先且带降级兜底。对 B 方向系统设计有直接指导意义。

### 🔥 #5 Agentic AI in Medicine: Challenges for Responsible Development and the Case for Clinical Testing Harnesses

- **期刊/来源**: Annals of Biomedical Engineering (2026)
- **作者**: Waisberg E, Guarnieri JW
- **标识**: DOI: `10.1007/s10439-026-04381-6` | PMID: `42776404`

> **一句话**: 提出智能体临床风险的评估单元应从"单次推理"转为"动作轨迹"，系统阐述四大挑战(静默错误跨步传播、只审结论不审过程、验证不随模型/工具更新存活、适用范围快于证据积累)，主张用"临床测试 harness"(场景库+全轨迹可观测+显式升级测试+分级证据阈值)而非发明新方法——复用医学已有的模拟/执照/M&M 复盘体系。

**摘要要点**: Agent scaffolding turns passive text generation into active, multi-step execution; the relevant unit of clinical risk therefore shifts from a single inference to a trajectory of actions. Four challenges: silent error propagation across multi-step tasks, oversight that reviews conclusions rather than processes, validation that does not survive changes to model or tooling, and scope that widens faster than the evidence. A clinical testing harness: scenario libraries from clinical edge cases, full-trajectory observability, explicit escalation testing, staged evidence thresholds tied to scope of practice.

**推荐理由**: B 方向智能体治理的框架性论文——"风险单元=轨迹而非推理"与"测试 harness 复用医学模拟/执照体系"两个论点尤其锋利，可作为我们评估自家 agent 系统的方法论参照。

## C. RLHF 医学对齐

### 🔥 #6 GRASP: Group-Adaptive Hybrid RL-SFT Post-Training for Medical VQA without Reasoning Annotations

- **期刊/来源**: arXiv (2026-10)
- **作者**: Wanqi Dong, Wanyue Dong, Jingze Ge, Mehul Motani
- **标识**: DOI: `10.48550/arxiv.2610.34561`

> **一句话**: 医学 VQA 缺推理标注场景下的 RL-SFT 混合后训练：每次更新同时做 RL(让模型自探索推理)与 SFT(推理后教金标答案补足 RL 缺的直接监督)，两者比例按每题模型表现自适应；仅 LoRA 调 ~1% 参数，在 Qwen3-VL-8B/InternVL3-8B/Gemma 3-4B 上域内与 OOD 开放问全面胜出。

**摘要要点**: SFT overfits to short-answer labels and amplifies bias; RL preserves generalization but GRPO-style learning gets no correctness signal from all-correct/all-wrong groups. GRASP combines RL and SFT in every update using only short answers, with balance adapting to per-question model performance. Tuning ~1% parameters with LoRA, outperforms all compared training objectives in-domain and on OOD open-ended questions across three VLM backbones.

**推荐理由**: 直击医学 RLHF 现实约束(无思维链标注、标注稀缺)，"每步混合+自适应配比"的设计比两阶段 SFT→RL 更精细；LoRA 1% 参数的低成本部署路径对医疗场景实用。

### 🔥 #7 MedNLI-Pain benchmarks a minimally necessary requirement for large language model alignment

- **期刊/来源**: JAMIA Open (2026)
- **作者**: Wong C, Kumar D, Muse A, Wang P, Wintz F, East SA, et al.
- **标识**: DOI: `10.1093/jamiaopen/ooag143` | PMID: `42840750`

> **一句话**: 在 MedNLI 临床推理基准上叠加"患者是否疼痛"的医师标注构成 MedNLI-Pain：所有 LLM 的临床推理一致率都显著高于疼痛预判一致率，且推理任务随训练数据增加而提升、疼痛预判不随数据增加改善——证明"推理对齐≠患者利益对齐"。

**摘要要点**: MedNLI-Pain evaluates if LLMs that make accurate clinical inferences can also anticipate when those patients are in pain. All LLMs had higher agreement with physicians in MedNLI than in MedNLI-Pain and its counterfactually augmented subset. Performance improved with more training data in MedNLI but not MedNLI-Pain. LLMs may lack necessary capabilities for clinical alignment; success at clinical reasoning may not entail commensurate ability to align with patients' interests.

**推荐理由**: 用"最小必要对齐"的巧妙设计把对齐研究从 benchmark 刷分拉回患者视角；"更多数据提升推理但不提升共情预判"是对 scaling 路线的直接质疑，值得 C 方向持续追踪。

### 🔥 #9 Biobank-scale self-supervised genotype pretraining yields transferable representations for human genetics

- **期刊/来源**: Nucleic Acids Research (2026)
- **作者**: Sheng C, Du H, Shen H, Dai H, Song F, Li X, Chen K
- **标识**: DOI: `10.1093/nar/gkag1059` | PMID: `42953967`

> **一句话**: SNPBERT——在 485,923 名 UK Biobank 参与者基因型上用 masked rsID prediction 自监督预训练的 Transformer；嵌入可恢复已知人群结构、支持祖源信息标记 SNP 识别，多实例注意力微调产生的疾病风险评分对常规 PRS 提供互补信息(Nagelkerke R² 相对提升 0.7%–3.3%)。

**摘要要点**: SNPBERT pretrained on genotypes from 485,923 UK Biobank participants using masked rsID prediction. Embeddings recovered known population structure across UK Biobank and three external datasets, performed comparably to PCA. Fine-tuning with attention-based multiple-instance head generated disease-specific risk scores complementary to conventional PRS (relative Nagelkerke R² increase 0.7%–3.3%; independent associations for 7 of 8 chronic diseases). "These results support biobank-scale genotype pretraining as an early step toward foundation modeling for individual-level genotype data."

**推荐理由**: 把基础模型范式从"序列"扩展到"个体级基因型"——与以 DNA 序列为中心的主流 D 方向形成互补；显式承认仍是"早期一步"，为后续个体级基因组 FM 立了标杆。

### 🔥 #10 Foundation model reveals the shared organization of transcription and topologically associating domains

- **期刊/来源**: Cell Systems (2026)
- **作者**: Liang H, Berger B, Singh R
- **标识**: DOI: `10.1016/j.cels.2026.101675` | PMID: `42468531`

> **一句话**: 用在 3,300 万个转录组上训练的基础模型定义"上下文相似度"度量(捕捉共表达 missed 的高阶关系)，发现 TAD 是共调控升高的区域——该增强在早期发育最强、随衰老衰退，癌细胞化疗后 TAD 使用改变，提示染色质组织以概率而非确定性机制起作用。

**摘要要点**: Species-level consensus TAD maps for human and mouse using a bag-of-genes approach. A foundation model trained on 33 million transcriptomes defines a contextual similarity metric capturing higher-order relationships missed by co-expression. TADs are regions of elevated co-regulation; TAD-linked enhancement strongest during early development, declines with aging, cancer cells show distinct TAD usage shifting with chemotherapy. Chromatin organization acts through probabilistic rather than deterministic mechanisms.

**推荐理由**: 基础模型不直接"预测变异"而是作为发现工具重塑三维基因组生物学认知——3,300 万转录组的规模与"概率性组织"的结论都新颖，是 D 方向与基因组学交叉的范例。

> **📎 候补提及 (D)**: DNA augmentations for semi-supervised learning in genomic sequence classification (NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics, DOI `10.1093/nargab/lqag123`, PMID 42898793)——以 GROVER DNA 语言模型为骨干，系统评测 25 组生物动机增强配方对 FixMatch/FlexMatch 半监督分类的影响：温和保序扰动最优，过度掩码/激增突变增大方差；短段掩码弱增强+~5% indel 强增强为最佳配方。做基因组 FM 下游半监督任务时可参考。

### 🔥🔥 #11 Toward truly agentic radiology AI: A multimodal architecture framework and differentiated implementation roadmap

- **期刊/来源**: European Journal of Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Irene Lee TL, Viswanathan VS, Kikano EG, Lall N, et al.
- **标识**: DOI: `10.1016/j.ejrad.2026.113318` | PMID: `42913348`

> **一句话**: 放射科智能体 AI 的架构+路线图论文：对比三种图文耦合架构(输出级串行流水线/表征级对比双编码器/Token级生成式 VLM)，提出 1–5 级自主度分级(从文本流程自动化到全自主闭环)并配套验证与监管检查点，强调 DICOMweb/HL7-FHIR/MCP/A2A 互操作标准与模型同等重要。

### 🔥 #12 OmniPathoVQA: Benchmarking pathology vision-language models with Encyclopedia-scale knowledge

- **作者**: Chen K, Wei L, Rui S, Yuan Y, et al.
- **标识**: DOI: `10.1016/j.media.2026.104196` | PMID: `42431152`

> **一句话**: 全人体系统、数千疾病实体的病理 VQA 基准：从病理教学材料细粒度抽取图文对齐，hard 版题目基于相似疾病的微解剖特征差异设计；评测 18 个 VLM 发现闭源强于开源但在难题上骤降，强通用推理能力是微调成功与病理特征集成的关键催化因子。

**推荐理由**: 补上病理 VLM 缺"知识密集型临床级评测"的短板，hard 题按微解剖特征区分设计避免了表面推理；18 模型横评结论(通用推理力>领域微调)对选型有直接参考价值。

### 🔥 #13 Large Language Models Provide Accurate but Potentially Unsafe Answers to Multimodal Critical Care Medicine Board Review Questions

- **作者**: Sethi I, Khan S, Lyons PG, Gao CA, et al. (含 L.A. Celi, C.M. Coopersmith, S.V. Bhavani)
- **标识**: DOI: `10.1097/CCM.0000000000007228` | PMID: `42307255`

> **一句话**: ChatGPT-4o 在 183 道重症医学 board 多模态题上正确率 74.9% 超过 pooled 临床医师的 71.1%(p=0.03)，但图像解读(61.7%)与推理(68.3%)显著弱于题目理解(87.4%)，14 名临床评审重点评估后指出潜在不安全回答——"更准但可能更不安全"。

### 🔥 #14 UniCure: A multi-modal model for predicting personalized cancer therapy response

- **期刊/来源**: Cancer Cell (2026)
- **作者**: Chen Z, Tian S, Pei J, Gu R, et al. (含 Q. Zhang, S. Ren, L. Chen, W. Zhang)
- **标识**: DOI: `10.1016/j.ccell.2026.07.010` | PMID: `42716007`

> **一句话**: X 方向(D×F 交叉)：生物+化学基础模型融合的多模态药物反应预测，在 190 万个转录组扰动谱(>22,000 化合物、166 细胞类型、24 组织)上训练，预测剂量依赖与联合用药反应并跨 bulk/单细胞泛化；在 345 例患者来源类簇转录组上微调、396 例真实世界临床谱验证，实现患者级个性化药物排序并获实验验证。

**摘要要点**: UniCure combines biological and chemical foundation models to predict drug-induced transcriptomic responses across diverse cell and tissue contexts, enabling individualized drug ranking. Trained on 1.9 million transcriptomic perturbation profiles spanning >22,000 compounds, 166 cell types, 24 tissues; predicts dose-dependent and combination responses, generalizes across bulk and single-cell data. Fine-tuned on 345 PTC profiles, validated on 396 real-world clinical profiles; experimentally validated in cell line and patient-derived models.

**推荐理由**: 生物基础模型×化学基础模型×真实世界临床数据的三重融合范式，把"预测药物反应"从细胞系推向患者级——是 D 方向基础模型走向临床决策(F)的完整闭环样本。

### 🔥 #15 Scaling genetic discovery through automated post-GWAS interpretation

- **期刊/来源**: bioRxiv 预印本 (2026-10-23)
- **作者**: Libo Qiao, Shuang Gu, Ye Yang, Yingnan Han, Xuanzhao Li
- **标识**: DOI: `10.64898/2026.10.23.761278`

> **一句话**: X 方向(B×D 交叉)：PGI 平台的多智能体引擎 VariantAgent 自动执行 post-GWAS 分析工作流，把结果记录为标准化 variant-centred 证据单元并生成可溯源的 trait 级解读；跨 34 项已发表 GWAS 平均恢复 82.1% 可比发现，对 1,041 项 GWAS 产出 530,949 个证据单元汇成跨性状目录，还发现了原研究未报告的调控关系。

**摘要要点**: PGI couples context-dependent post-GWAS analysis to a common representation of analysis-derived evidence. VariantAgent executes workflows, records tool-derived results in standardized variant-centred units, generates trait-level interpretations linked to analytical basis and evidential status. Outperformed general-purpose and biomedical agents, with largest advantage when success required both correct answer and valid analytical trace. Across 34 published GWAS, mean per-study recovery 82.1%. Analysis of 1,041 GWAS yielded 530,949 evidence units organized into a cross-trait catalogue.

**推荐理由**: B 方向"分析型智能体"与 D 方向遗传学的罕见高价值交叉——"正确答案+有效分析轨迹"的双重要求设计与 BT-RADS(#3)的轨迹评估思想共振；跨性状证据目录展示了智能体规模化生产可积累科学证据的路径。

## 🧠 前沿洞察与头脑风暴

本期三个信号值得放在一起读：#1(mNGS 病原裁决 LLM)、#3(BT-RADS 多智能体评分)、#15(VariantAgent post-GWAS)本质上是同一件事——**智能体正在接管临床/科研流程中的"裁决环节"，而非"诊断环节"**。mNGS 报告解读、影像随访分级、GWAS 位点解读的共同特征是：原始检测/关联早已自动化，卡住人力的是"把结构化结果映射到决策"的裁决步骤。这类环节容错结构清晰(有金标、有规则、可回溯)，恰是当前 agent 最容易兑现价值的落点。建议后续检索显式加入"adjudication / scoring / interpretation agent"类词，而不是只搜"clinical agent"。

第二，**"轨迹级评估"正在成为共识**：#5 提出风险单元=动作轨迹、需要测试 harness；#15 的评测优势恰恰来自"答案正确+分析轨迹有效"的双重要求；#3 的多智能体系统也是抽取器+打分器分工可审计。可以预期 2027 年会出现专门的 agent 轨迹评测标准(类似 SWE-bench 之于代码智能体)，值得提前布局内部轨迹日志与回放基建。

第三，**D 方向出现"从序列 FM 到系统 FM"的迁移**：#9(SNPBERT，个体级基因型)、#10(3,300 万转录组训练的组织相似度 FM)、#14(生物×化学 FM 融合)共同显示基础模型的输入正在从"一条 DNA 序列"扩展到"一个个体的组学全景"。白地提示：**表观组(甲基化/染色质可及性)级别的基础模型仍稀缺**，且临床多模态智能体(F)与这些基因组 FM 之间几乎没有桥接——"FM 输出作为 agent 工具"是明确空白。

策略建议：① C 方向(RLHF 医学对齐)本期 PubMed 产出仍最薄，纯 RLHF 医疗论文多发 arXiv，建议对 C 方向增加 arXiv cs.CL 检索权重；② A 方向可加搜 "report interpretation / clinical significance calling" 等词以持续捕获 #1 这类"解读裁决"论文；③ medRxiv/bioRxiv 新 DOI 前缀 10.64898 本期贡献 3 篇，务必保持 DataCite 核验路径。
