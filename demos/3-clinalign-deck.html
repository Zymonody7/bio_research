Problem → Approach

PROBLEM
Coarse Alignment
Existing medical LLMs rely on coarse objectives or unreliable auto-judges. Open-ended clinical responses fail fine-grained clinician alignment.
INSIGHT
Rubrics as Bridge
Clinician-verified rubrics capture granular medical standards. Distill rubrics into reusable principles for scalable supervision.
SOLUTION
Two-Stage Framework
Stage 1: Collect physician-verified rubrics (HealthRubrics). Stage 2: Distill into 119 reusable principles (HealthPrinciples).

Stage 1: HealthRubrics

STEP 1
Medical Curation
Aggregate from Chatbot Arena (human-140k, human-55k, expert-5k), HelpSteer3. GPT-5.1 classifies → 7,034 medical instances.
STEP 2
Physician Consensus
111 physicians across specialties. 3 re-labels per instance. 55.2% unanimous agreement (Kappa=0.47). Physician-user match only 0.60–0.64.
STEP 3
Iterative Refinement
GPT-5.1 drafts rubric → 2 physicians review in succession. Avg 1.34 loops (75.3% accepted in 1). Cost: 632 hrs / $15,172.

Key Takeaways

1. Rubrics > Raw Preferences

Granular rubrics capture clinical reasoning dimensions that binary preferences miss. 119 principles enable scalable, reusable supervision.

2. Small Can Beat Large

30B-A3B outperforms DeepSeek-R1 and o3. Method matters more than model size for clinical alignment.

3. Doctor Involvement Scales

$15k and 632 hours → reusable principles that benefit unlimited future queries. One-time cost, perpetual alignment signal.

4. For Your RLHF Project

Rubric-based annotation design, dual online/offline principle use, and the taxonomy framework are directly applicable to your doctor-facing UI.
